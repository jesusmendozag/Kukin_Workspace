# Kukin app (all screens)

Scope: every screen of the Capacitor Android app (login, inventario, añadir, para ti, detalle, dietas, tú). Mode: Operate.

Audience/job: foráneo students and Mexican/Latina moms deciding what to cook from what they have, one-handed in the kitchen. Constraints: full edge-to-edge screen with native insets (--safe-*), usable with keyboard open, big type/targets, high contrast, emoji per ingredient, add flow = grid of common ingredients + search.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Kukin's own organic, editorial, tactile system, kept and improved: a warm cream kitchen notebook with white cards, editorial Fraunces titles and basil-green actions. Refuses re-inventing the identity.

OWN-WORLD: Cream #FDFBF7 ground (sand #F7F3EE on login), white cards rounded 20–24px, containers 32–40px; basil #2D7A47 actions, navy #0B1E36 titles and internal actions; fresh/alert/amber/discover state colours; pastel emoji corners on ingredient cards; Fraunces black display, system sans UI, uppercase tracked micro-labels.

STORY: Opens on "Mi inventario", sees what expires first, sees a "Para ti" card with ready recipes, taps the tilted + to add by tapping emoji cards, opens a recipe whose match badge and "Te falta" chips explain it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact Fraunces title "Mi inventario" with count on cream; lavender "Para ti" card; filter pills; 2-column white ingredient cards with pastel emoji corner, X top-left, freshness pill; green rotate-3 FAB above the floating white pill nav.
